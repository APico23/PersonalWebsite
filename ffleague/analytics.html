Consistency and Matchup Luck

Consistency rating starts at 100 and subtracts the team's average weekly distance from its own mean score. Higher is steadier; lower means more boom-or-bust. It measures predictability, not scoring strength.

Luck differential adds 1 for each head-to-head win and subtracts 1 for each loss. Positive means the schedule produced more wins than losses; negative means the opposite. Read it beside average points, because it is not a manager-skill grade.
